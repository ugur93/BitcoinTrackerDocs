Mayer Multiple: Bitcoin price vs its 200-day average

The Mayer Multiple is the Bitcoin price divided by its 200-day simple moving average. It tells you how stretched the price is compared with the last seven months.

Historical zones

  • Below 0.8: deep discount. Historically rare, and it has marked cheap periods.
  • 0.8 to 1.5: normal range.
  • 1.5 to 2.4: elevated. The price is well above its trend.
  • Above 2.4: overheated. Past cycle tops happened here.

Pi Cycle Top

A related signal compares the 111-day average with twice the 350-day average. When the 111-day line crosses above, it has lined up with previous cycle tops within a few days. Like the Mayer Multiple it looks backwards and can fail in a new cycle.

Limits

Both indicators describe where the price is relative to its own past. They are useful context, not timing tools, and this page is not financial advice.
