Bitcoin power law: fair value today

The power-law model fits Bitcoin's whole price history to a single straight line on a log-log chart. It gives a "fair value" for any date and a floor the price has rarely closed below.

The formula

The version used here and in the Bitcoin Tracker app:

fair value (USD) = 1.0117 × 10-17 × days5.82

where days is the number of days since the genesis block on 3 January 2009. Because the exponent is fixed, the model's growth rate slows every year: it rose roughly 40% a year in the early 2020s and keeps falling from there.

How to read it

  • Above fair value: the price has run ahead of the long-term trend. Cycle tops have sat several times above the line.
  • Near the floor (0.42×): bear-market lows have clustered here. The price has rarely closed below it.
  • Between the two: most of Bitcoin's history.

Limits

The power law is a curve fitted to the past, not a law of nature. It says nothing about when the price will move, and a fit can break. Use it as one lens next to others, such as the Mayer Multiple, and never as a sole reason to buy or sell. This page is not financial advice.
